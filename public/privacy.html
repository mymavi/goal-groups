Privacy

What we keep, and why.

Short version: a first name, an email and a town. We don’t sell any of it.

What we collect

  • When you post a goal: your first name, email, town (optional), the goal itself, its dates and crew settings.
  • When you spot or join a goal: your first name and email.
  • Check-ins: whether you tapped Saw it or Didn’t happen, and when.
  • For spam limits only: a scrambled, daily-changing code made from your IP address. We never store the IP itself.

What other people see

Your first name, town, goal, spotter first names, check-in totals and show-up rate. Never your email.

Why

To show goals on the board, let spotters check in, and work out each person’s show-up rate. Your email ties your goals to one record, and lets us reach you about a goal you posted or spot if we need to.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or rent your info, and we don’t share it with advertisers. No newsletters unless you ask for them.

Where it lives

On Cloudflare, where this site is hosted. Your browser also keeps a private key for goals you posted and goals you spot, so you can manage them from that device.

Delete your info

Email jamenrk@gmail.com from the address you used and we’ll delete your goals, spotter sign-ups and check-ins. You can also remove a goal you posted from its page, on the device you posted it from.
