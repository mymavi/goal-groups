About this preview

This is a preview.

An early, unlisted version of Goal Groups. The board is real and shared, but small.

What gets shared

Goals you post go on a shared board. Spotters and crew show by first name. Emails stay private. Full details are on the privacy page.

Example content

Pins marked Example, and the sample profiles and creator pages, are fictional. They show how things work until more real goals are up.

What we show about you

A first name and a town, never an address. Spotters see your check-ins, not your location.
